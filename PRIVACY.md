# Privacy Policy: YouTube AI Agent

Last updated: 2026-09-30

YouTube AI Agent is a browser extension that runs entirely in your browser. It has no server of its own, and the developer does not collect, receive, or store any of your data.

## What the extension handles

- **Your AI provider settings** (provider, API key, model, endpoint URL, preferences) are stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.
- **Video content**: when you ask for a summary, explanation, or commentary, the extension reads the open video's title, channel, description, tags, and captions from youtube.com.
- **Your prompts and chat history**: what you type, and the answers you get, are saved per video in Chrome's local extension storage on your device so they are there when you return. The 🗑 button deletes a video's chat. Removing the extension deletes everything.

## Where data is sent

The video content and your prompts are sent only to the AI provider you choose in settings (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or a custom endpoint you enter), using your own API key. That provider's privacy policy applies to what it receives. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- No selling or sharing of data.
- No access to websites other than youtube.com, the AI provider you picked, and any custom endpoint you approve.
